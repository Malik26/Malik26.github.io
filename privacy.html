Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Kids Shorts Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the GigglePlaytime YouTube channel. It is used only to upload our own videos to our own channel.

What the app accesses

The app requests a single Google permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store any other data from Google accounts or from YouTube viewers.

How data is stored

The OAuth token issued by Google is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared, sold or sent to any third party.

Children

Our videos are made for kids. The app does not collect any personal information from children or any viewer.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
